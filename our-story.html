Our Story

From One Shelf to Thousands

How a recommendation list between friends became an online bookstore shipping to readers nationwide.

BookHaven started in 2021 as a weekend project between three friends who kept recommending books to each other. That recommendation list became a curated webshop; the webshop became a warehouse; the warehouse now ships to readers nationwide.

A lot has changed — but the heart hasn't. We still read before we recommend, we still hunt for the best price on every title, and we still pack every order as if it were a gift for a friend.
