Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, returns and getting in touch with the BookHaven team.

Help Center

How do I create a BookHaven account?
Click the account icon at the top of any page and choose “Sign up”. You only need an email address and a password. An account lets you check out faster, track orders and keep a wishlist.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
On the sign-in screen, use the “Forgot password” option and follow the instructions sent to your email. If you no longer have access to that email, contact us and we will help you recover your account.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major debit and credit cards, bank transfer and cash on delivery within supported areas. All online payments are processed over an encrypted connection.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders can be changed or cancelled while they are still being processed. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we will do our best — once a parcel has shipped, changes are no longer possible.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on your location. Major cities usually receive orders within 2–3 days; remote areas may take up to 7 business days.
What does shipping cost?
Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your address and order size. Orders above the free-shipping threshold shown on the cart page qualify for free standard delivery.
Do you deliver outside major cities?
Yes — we deliver nationwide through our courier partners. Exact availability and delivery estimates for your area are shown at checkout before you pay.
How is my order packaged?
Every book is wrapped in protective padding and shipped in a sturdy mailer so it reaches you in the same condition it left our shelves.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
You can return most items within 14 days of delivery. Books must be unused and in resellable condition. Simply contact us with your order number to start a return.
My book arrived damaged. What should I do?
We are sorry about that! Send us a photo of the damage within 48 hours of delivery and we will send a replacement or issue a full refund — including any shipping fees.
When will I get my refund?
Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after the returned item reaches us. Your bank may take a few extra days to post the money to your statement.

Track Your Order

How can I track my order?
Once your parcel ships we email you a tracking number and link. You can also sign in, open your account page and view the status of every order you have placed.
My tracking has not updated in a while. Is something wrong?
Tracking can lag during weekends, holidays or long-distance legs of the route. If there is no movement for more than 3 business days, reach out with your order number and we will investigate with the courier.

Contact Us

How do I reach customer support?
Email us at support@bookhaven.example and include your order number where relevant. We reply within one business day, Monday to Saturday.
What are your support hours?
Our team is available Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm, and Saturdays 10am–4pm. Messages received outside these hours are answered the next working day.
Do you have a physical store?
BookHaven is primarily an online bookstore, which keeps our prices low. Selected titles appear at partner bookshops — write to us and we will point you to the nearest one.
